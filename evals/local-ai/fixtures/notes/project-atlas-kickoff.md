# Project Atlas — kickoff meeting

Date: 9 March 2026 · Location: Room 4B, Brightwell Logistics HQ

## Attendees
- Ana Costa (product)
- Marcus Oyelaran (engineering lead)
- Priya Nandakumar (finance)
- Liam Fairbrook (customer support)

## Context
Atlas replaces the legacy billing system ("Ledger 2") with the new invoicing platform. Ledger 2 licences expire at the end of July 2026.

## Decisions
- Go-live date: **30 June 2026**
- Approved budget: **$48,000** (contingency included)
- Data migration runs in two waves: active customers first, archived accounts second
- Marcus owns the technical cut-over plan

## Discussion
- Priya flagged that the budget excludes the external audit (~$6,500), which finance will cover separately.
- Liam asked for a two-week freeze on billing changes before go-live.
- Ana proposed weekly status emails every Thursday.

## Open questions

- Who signs off the migration of archived accounts?
