# The Quiet Orchard — Helena Marsh (2019)

Rating: 4/5 · Read in January 2026

## Premise
A retired botanist, Edwin Hale, inherits his sister's failing apple orchard in rural Wales and spends one year trying to save it.

## Themes
- Grief as slow work, like grafting
- Patience versus control
- Inherited responsibilities

## Quotes
> "You cannot hurry a tree; you can only stop hurting it."

## Thoughts
- The middle third drags, but the winter chapters are beautiful.
- The ending (Edwin sells half the land to a cider co-op) felt earned.
- Recommend to Dad.
