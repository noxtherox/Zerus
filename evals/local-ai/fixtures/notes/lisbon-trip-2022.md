# Lisbon long weekend 2022

With Tom Reyes, **6–9 October 2022**.

- Stayed at **Pensão Alfama Velha** — €95/night, 3 nights
- Budget: €800 total
- Flew with VZ 211 from Bristol, back on the Sunday evening
- Highlights: sunset at Miradouro de Santa Luzia, seafood at Cervejaria Doca Nova
- Rained all of Saturday, so we skipped Sintra
- Lost my umbrella on tram 28 :(

Next time: stay in Graça, go to Sintra, book a fado dinner in advance.
